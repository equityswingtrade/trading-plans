Warsh — what he said, and how gold reacted (this report, at your request)

What he said (a fireside chat with IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva in Bangkok, 22:30 CT):

  • He declined to give any signal on an October hike. He said he does not "prejudge meetings" and would not announce in advance what the Fed might do at its remaining 2026 meetings.
  • He described a "synchronous tightening" by central banks worldwide ("no free riders"), and said the Fed had "made very clear we'd deliver price stability."
  • He repeated that AI should lift productivity over the medium term, on a path he called "very bumpy."
  • How the market read it: as relief, not a hawkish turn. Press reports put the implied odds of a hike at the 27–28 October meeting down from about 27–30% to about 15–17%, and the greenback softened. For gold that is the real-rate channel at work: fewer hikes priced and a weaker dollar. Goldman Sachs economists read it as a pause in October and a hike in December.
