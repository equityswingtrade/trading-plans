1. PROPOSED — a Rule 7(b) exemption when overnight inventory is extended

The conflict this morning. Rule 7(b) says a favoured-LONG ★ must sit at or above price (≥ −0.25× ATR). Rule 15 says that when overnight inventory is ≥1× the 30-min ATR, the correction comes first — so the favoured long should be bought on the pullback, below price. On ES and NQ today (2.65× and 2.72×) the two rules point at opposite ★ levels:

7(b) literally would star Rule 15 says buy at This run starred
ES 7762.50 or 7768.00 (a break-long) the 7752.50 node 7752.50
NQ 30266.75 or 30342.50 (a break-long) the 30155.75 node 30155.75

How I resolved it for this run: with the skill's existing precedence rule — "A house rule may not contradict a Market-Dynamics law or the playbook. When it does, the reference wins." Rule 15 is Tier A (Playbook §3b); 7(b) is a Tier-B house rule. And 7(b)'s own stated purpose is to avoid starring a level that needs "a counter-move — one the thesis itself says should not happen" — today the thesis says the counter-move should happen. Every level 7(b) would have chosen still has its own alert.

The proposed permanent wording (for your approval):

"Rule 7(b) exemption: when Rule 15 measures overnight inventory ≥ 1× the 30-min ATR in the direction of the favoured branch, the ★ may sit on the correction side of price, up to the Rule 7 gate (1.0×). The correction is the thesis, so a ★ at the correction's support is not the failure 7(b) guards against. The break-level in the favoured direction keeps its own alert."

⚠ The honest risk: if there is no correction today, ES's and NQ's ★s won't fire — exactly Friday's ES miss. The break-long alerts (ES 7768.00, NQ 30266.75) are live to cover that case. Approve, reject, or amend.
