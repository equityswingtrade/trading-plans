1. NEW — Candidate Rule 22: at an extended open, rank the CONTINUATION of the correction, not the FADE of it (logged, NOT adopted)

The problem. Rule 15 currently says that when inventory exceeds ~1× the 30-min ATR, "the inventory correction comes FIRST — DEMOTE the go-with BREAK below the fade and rotation scenarios." That sentence contains two different claims and only one of them is supported:

  • (a) the first move will be a correction — Thursday supports this completely;
  • (b) therefore the trade is to fade that correction at the first resistance — Thursday contradicts this completely.

I applied the rule literally on all three symbols and got (a) right and (b) wrong three times in one session.

Proposed wording: at an extended open, the correction itself is the ranked trade. RANK 1 becomes the go-WITH of the corrective move (long the bounce on a short-extended open), with T1 placed at the level the fade would otherwise have been placed at; the fade of that move drops to RANK 3 and requires a confirming 30-min close back through the level, not a touch.

The evidence FOR — 2026-09-24:

Open inventory Where I placed the fade Did the fade work? What the correction actually did

The evidence AGAINST — and I think it is stronger than it looks:

  1. ⚠ One session across three correlated instruments is arguably n=1, not n=3. All three sold on the same summit headline and all three turned on the same risk-on reversal. Rule 4 says ES and NQ are one position; the same logic says this is close to one observation.
  2. ⚠ Rule 15's own worked example is a counter-case. ES 2026-08-27 opened +1.24× long and the first RTH bar flushed straight to 7702.75 before the trend resumed — the correction came and fading it was the right first trade. Adopting Rule 22 as written would have been wrong that day.
  3. ⚠ The open-drive log is now exactly 50/50 (see §3 below) — three cases drove, three corrected. That is not a basis for reversing a rule's direction.
  4. ⚠ The threshold may be the real problem, not the direction. GC was only −1.15× and behaved like NQ at −2.48×; if the flip applied above 2× but not below, today fits and 08-27 (+1.24×) does not contradict it.

My recommendation: do NOT adopt as written. If you want to act on this, the narrower version is defensible: at ≥2.0× extension, rank the continuation of the correction first; between 1.0× and 2.0×, leave Rule 15 exactly as it is. That fits ES and NQ today, leaves GC as an honest miss, and does not contradict 08-27. ⛔ Nothing has been written to the skill. Your call.
