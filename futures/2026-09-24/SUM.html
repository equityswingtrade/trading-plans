Proposed wording:

"Rule 21: when the trigger is jumped by the RTH OPENING PRINT rather than by a bar's travel, the max-chase band is measured from the OPEN, not from the trigger. The entry is then the first 30-min bar that fails to reclaim the trigger — enter on its close, stop 0.5× ATR beyond that bar's extreme. Why it is not obviously right: it re-opens exactly the behaviour Rule 18 exists to stop (chasing), and the 09-08 case that cost 0.95R was a gap-ish open too. I am not applying it.
