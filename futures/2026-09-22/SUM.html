2. NOT ADOPTED — Rule 15(b): the open-drive exception (your call, 09-21 — Rule 15 stays as written; kept below for the record)

The "corrections" were 2.25 and 5.50 points. Both markets drove from the first bar. Rule 15's record: 3-for-4 → 3-for-6. Playbook §3b already names the exception: an open-drive is the one open type in which inventory does not correct.

Proposed wording:

*"Rule 15(b): if the first RTH 30-min bar closes in the direction of the inventory, and its opposite extreme holds within ~0.25× the 30-min ATR of the open, it is an open-drive — cancel the demotion; the go-with break is live. And in a pre-session plan, Rule 15 may demote a break's RANK and widen stops, but it may never move the ★ off the side Rule 7(b) requires."*

Decision: not adopted. Today's reports apply Rule 15 as written; the open-drive note in each §4 table is an observation only.
